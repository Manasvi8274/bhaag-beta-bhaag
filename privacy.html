Bhaag Beta Bhaag! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Summary

Bhaag Beta Bhaag! has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store any personal information. The Android app offers an optional video ad (you choose to watch it to escape Papa); that ad is provided by Google AdMob, described below.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress the game saves a small record in your device's local storage: your best score, coin balance, unlocked outfits, missions, daily-reward streak and your sound/vibration settings. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Network access

The web version downloads its font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request (see Google's privacy policy). The game works offline after the first load.

Advertising (Android app)

Ads are shown only when you tap "Watch ad to escape". They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to deliver, measure and (where allowed) personalise ads. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. In regions that require it (such as the EU/UK) the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

Permissions

The Android app uses vibration for gameplay feedback (can be turned off in the pause menu). Internet access is used to load optional ads; the advertising ID permission is used by AdMob.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. Ads are limited to content suitable for a parental-guidance rating.

Changes
