Bhaag Beta Bhaag! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Bhaag Beta Bhaag! has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store any personal information. The Android app offers optional video ads (you choose to watch one to escape Papa or to double a run's coins); those ads are provided by Google AdMob, described below. The web version offers the same optional ads through Google AdSense (H5 Games Ads). If you choose to challenge a friend or play a live race, the nickname you typed and your run are sent to the other players, as described under "Playing with others".

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress the game saves a small record in your device's local storage: your best score, coin balance, unlocked outfits, missions, daily-reward streak, your sound/vibration settings and the nickname you typed (if any). This record stays on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Playing with others

Challenge links. When you tap "Challenge a friend" or "Share", the game makes a link containing the street you ran, your distance and your nickname, and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is sent unless you share it.

Live races (Muqabla). While you are in a race room, your position on the street and whether you have been caught are sent to the other players in that room, several times a second. In a room joined with a code your nickname is sent too; in a Quick Match with a random player no nickname is sent or shown. These messages pass through a public message relay (EMQX public MQTT broker, or the Eclipse Mosquitto test broker as a backup), which receives your IP address as part of the connection. The messages are not encrypted for a particular room, so do not use your real name as a nickname if you would not want strangers to see it. The developer does not store these messages. Nothing is sent when you are not in a room.

Network access

The web version downloads its font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request (see Google's privacy policy). The game works offline after the first load.

Advertising

Ads are shown only when you tap an ad button ("Watch ad to escape" or "2× coins"). They are served by Google AdMob (Android app) or Google AdSense (web version), which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to deliver, measure and (where allowed) personalise ads. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. In regions that require it (such as the EU/UK) the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

Permissions

The Android app uses vibration for gameplay feedback (can be turned off in the pause menu). Internet access is used to load optional ads and for live races; the advertising ID permission is used by AdMob.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. Ads are limited to content suitable for a parental-guidance rating.
